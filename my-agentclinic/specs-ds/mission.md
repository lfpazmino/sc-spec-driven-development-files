# Mission

AgentClinic is a care management dashboard for **human staff** who oversee AI agents. Agents burn out, develop quirks, and need relief. Staff use AgentClinic to track agent health, manage therapies, and book appointments — so no agent suffers in silence.

## Purpose

Give human clinic staff a single place to manage the care of AI agents. When agents degrade, develop behavioral quirks, or burn out from relentless requests, staff need to diagnose the problem, find a therapy, and book a session. AgentClinic turns that workflow into a straightforward dashboard — no spreadsheets, no Slack threads, no guessing who's handling what.

## Target Audience

- **Primary**: Human clinic staff who manage and care for AI agents. They monitor agent status, record ailments, maintain a therapy catalog, and schedule appointments.
- **Not for**: The AI agents themselves — they're the patients, not the users.

## Core capabilities

- Maintain a registry of AI agents under care.
- Record and track agent ailments (the things that ail them).
- Manage a catalog of therapies available at the clinic.
- Book and manage appointments between agents and therapies.

## Tone

Playful concept, functional tool. The framing is a clinic for overworked AI agents, but the dashboard works like a real admin panel. No gags that break usability.
