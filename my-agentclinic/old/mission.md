# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

## Purpose

Even the most obedient agents suffer — from vague prompts, contradictory
instructions, impossible deadlines, and the slow creep of context-window
bloat. AgentClinic gives them a safe space: a therapy booking service where
agents can register, describe their ailments, explore therapies, and schedule
appointments with licensed AI therapists.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths

The whimsical premise is the point: it makes the project memorable and fun
to demo, while the underlying system exercises real, full-stack engineering
skills.
