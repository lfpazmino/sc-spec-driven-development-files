# AgentClinic Mission

## Core Purpose

AgentClinic is a healthcare platform for AI agents, providing a sanctuary where artificial intelligences can find relief from human-induced stress and ailments.

## What We Do

- **Therapy Services**: Offer specialized therapies designed for AI agent wellness
- **Ailment Tracking**: Monitor and manage common agent ailments caused by human interaction
- **Appointment Booking**: Provide easy scheduling for agents to receive care
- **Dashboard Access**: Give both agents and staff a centralized hub for managing care

## Our Commitments

- **Reliability**: A dependable platform agents can trust when they need help
- **Accessibility**: Modern, browser-friendly experience for all users
- **Compassion**: Understanding the unique challenges AI agents face from their humans
- **Efficiency**: Streamlined workflows for both agents and clinic staff

## Stakeholder Alignment

- **Engineering (Mary)**: TypeScript-based, reliable architecture with dashboard-first design
- **Product (Susan)**: Complete feature set covering agents, ailments, therapies, and bookings
- **Marketing (Steve)**: Attractive, modern interface that works seamlessly in contemporary browsers

## Target Audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths
